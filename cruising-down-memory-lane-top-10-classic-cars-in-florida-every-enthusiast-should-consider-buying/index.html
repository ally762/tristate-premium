Cruising’ Down Memory Lane: Top 10 Classic Cars in Florida Every Enthusiast Should Consider Buying

Home/Blog/Cruising’ Down Memory Lane: Top 10 Classic Cars in Florida Every Enthusiast Should Consider Buying
adminOctober 16, 2023Car Restoration

If you’re a classic car enthusiast in sunny Florida, you’re in for a treat! The Sunshine State is a haven for vintage car lovers, and we’ve compiled a list of the top 10 classic cars that should be on your radar if you want to buy or get classic car restoration in Florida.

So, buckle up and get ready to explore the classics that will turn heads.

1. 1969 Chevrolet Camaro SS

Qualities: The ’69 Camaro SS is an American icon known for its muscular stance and powerful V8 engine. With its aggressive design and thrilling performance, it’s a dream for any muscle car enthusiast.

Specs: The SS has a 396 cubic-inch V8 engine, delivering 375 horsepower. It’s available with automatic and manual transmissions, offering a driving experience that suits your preference.

Price Range: Depending on condition and rarity, you can find ’69 Camaro SS models in Florida ranging from $40,000 to $80,000.

2. 1957 Chevrolet Bel Air

Qualities: The ’57 Bel Air is a symbol of American automotive elegance. Its iconic tailfins and chrome details make it a timeless classic that exudes style and nostalgia.

Specs: Typically powered by a V8 engine, this beauty offers a smooth ride and is often equipped with options like power steering and air conditioning for comfort. If you get it restored by the best car restoration shops in FL, you are in for a treat.

Price Range: Prices for a ’57 Bel Air in Florida can range from $40,000 to $70,000 or more, depending on the condition.

3. 1965 Ford Mustang

Qualities: The Ford Mustang is a legend that needs no introduction. The ’65 model is a pony car masterpiece, combining style and affordability, making it one of the most beloved classics.

Specs: It features a range of engines, from the inline-six to the potent V8, providing a thrilling driving experience. Its iconic design and customizable options make it a versatile classic.

Price Range: You can find ’65 Mustangs in Florida starting at around $20,000 and going up to $50,000 or more for rare versions.

4. 1970 Dodge Challenger R/T

Qualities: The ’70 Challenger R/T is a true muscle car icon. With its bold, aggressive design and powerful engines, it’s an adrenaline rush on wheels that turns heads wherever it goes.

Specs: Equipped with a range of V8 engine options, the R/T is all about raw power. It’s a beast on the road, delivering heart-pounding acceleration.

Price Range: In Florida, prices for a ’70 Challenger R/T can vary widely, starting from around $50,000 and going up to $100,000 or higher.

5. 1963 Porsche 356B

Qualities: The ’63 Porsche 356B is a gem if European classics are more your speed. Its timeless design, nimble handling, and air-cooled flat-four engine make it a classic sports car.

Specs: With a focus on precision, the 356B offers a heavenly driving experience. It’s a true testament to Porsche’s commitment to performance.

Price Range: Prices for a ’63 Porsche 356B in Florida can range from $50,000 to $100,000 or more.

6. 1959 Cadillac Eldorado

Qualities: The ’59 Cadillac Eldorado epitomizes American luxury and excess. Its iconic tailfins, acres of chrome, and plush interior define the era of classic American cars.

Specs: Powered by a V8 engine, the Eldorado offers a smooth and comfortable ride. It’s a statement of luxury.

Price Range: In Florida, you can find ’59 Eldorado models ranging from $40,000 to $80,000 or more.

7. 1967 Shelby Cobra 427

Qualities: The ’67 Shelby Cobra 427 is a legend for those seeking raw, unbridled power. It’s a pure sports car known for its lightweight construction and roaring V8 engine.

Specs: With a massive 7-liter V8 engine, this Cobra delivers uncanny acceleration and an exhilarating driving experience.

Price Range: Prices for a ’67 Shelby Cobra 427 in Florida can start at around $1 million and go significantly higher for well-preserved originals or replicas.

8. 1976 Volkswagen Beetle

Qualities: The ’76 Volkswagen Beetle may not have the same power as some of the muscle cars, but it holds a special place in the hearts of classic car enthusiasts.

Specs: The Beetle is powered by an air-cooled flat-four engine, providing reliable and economical transportation with a touch of nostalgia.

Price Range: You can find ’76 Volkswagen Beetles in Florida for as little as $5,000 to $15,000 or more.

9. 1969 Jaguar E-Type

Qualities: The ’69 Jaguar E-Type is a British classic epitomizing elegance and performance. With its long hood and sleek lines, it’s often hailed as one of the most beautiful cars ever made.

Specs: Powered by a six-cylinder engine, the E-Type offers a thrilling driving experience, combining luxury and performance in a harmonious package.

Price Range: Prices for a ’69 Jaguar E-Type in Florida can range from $60,000 to $150,000.

10. 1964 Chevrolet Corvette Stingray

Qualities: Last but certainly not least, we have the ’64 Corvette Stingray. This iconic American sports car boasts stunning looks and impressive performance, making it a classic that demands attention. Classic car restorers in FL work carefully to keep the beauty alive in this masterpiece

Specs: Equipped with V8 engines, the Stingray offers power and agility. Its timeless design and unmistakable presence make it a true classic.

Price Range: In Florida, you can find ’64 Corvette Stingrays starting at around $40,000 and going up to $80,000 or more.
