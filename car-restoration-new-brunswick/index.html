Comprehensive Automotive Restoration in New Brunswick

We understand that every vehicle has a story and we’re here to help you continue yours. Our automotive restoration in New Brunswick includes everything from full frame-off restorations to partial upgrades. Our services include:

  • Engine and drivetrain rebuilds
  • Interior restoration and upholstery

  • Rust removal and undercoating
  • Custom paint and detailing
  • Classic car parts sourcing and installation

Whether you’re restoring a Corvette, Mustang, or a vintage truck, our team ensures that your car is returned in showroom condition ready to turn heads at the next car show or weekend cruise.
